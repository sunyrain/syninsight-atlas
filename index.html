Why this dataset

Route value is more than step count

01

Evidence before admission

Paper identity, target stereochemistry, and route-defining transformations remain separate claims with source locators.

02

Insight before imitation

Literature similarity and intrinsic route value are evaluated separately, so credible novel strategies remain visible.

03

Failures stay in the denominator

Unresolved structures, missing sources, and negative reviews are first-class dataset states rather than hidden exclusions.
