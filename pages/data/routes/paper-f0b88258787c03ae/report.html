  • Article Scheme 1 is retrosynthesis; Scheme 4 includes proposed epoxide 16 and unsuccessful experiments. Neither 16 nor speculative enolates are represented as obtained products.
  • The source draws unspecified epoxide stereochemistry and reports 2:1 dr. Both epoxide centers remain unspecified; no major/minor configuration is assigned.
  • 9 is reported at 93:7 er; its SMILES identifies the named major enantiomer. For 15 the article distinguishes 3:1 crude dr from isolated major product. Purity is not inferred for downstream samples.

  • SI procedure uses THF and aqueous NaOH, whereas the scheme and main text say MeOH/H2O. Structured solvent field follows the procedure and retains the conflict.
  • SI gives 1.11 g and 3.19 mmol for iodide 12; these do not match its formula C15H23IO3 (about 378.25 g/mol). Source identity and HRMS support the iodine graph; amount data are not repaired.
  • Natural-product reference marker 4 in SI comparison headings is a literature citation, not the labels of Rhodocoranes D/E. Product labels are 5/6.
