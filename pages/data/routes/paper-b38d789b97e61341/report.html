  • SI page 8 calls the precursor 3. The drawn cyclic product, lactam reduction and reported precursor mass/mmol identify 4 (365.5 g/mol), not acyclic 3 (393.6 g/mol). This is an explicit interpretation pending review.
  • SI page 9 calls the starting compound 13, also the product label. The reported starting mass/mmol matches secondary amine 3; N,N-dimethyl quaternization is represented as 3 to 13, pending review.
  • SI page 6 draws formic acid beside 4 but prints C24H33N3O2. Parent plus one formic acid would contain O3. The route uses the covalent parent with adduct metadata, without assigning a protonation site or corrected isolated formula.
  • SI page 10 HRMS formula C16H24N disagrees with drawing C15H23N and m/z 218.1903, which match protonated C15H24N. Both source and graph-derived formulas are retained.
  • SI labels neutral formulas C13H27NO2 and C21H31NO as [M+H]+. The reported masses match their protonated ions. Neutral formulas are not treated as ion formulas.
  • Reported yield is 23%; the amount of amine 17 is absent. Yield is transcribed, not recomputed from the separately given acid amount.
  • Wavy tetrahedral bonds remain unspecified; 4 is an E/Z 7:1 mixture. No E/Z ratio is inferred for final 2 from its precursor.
