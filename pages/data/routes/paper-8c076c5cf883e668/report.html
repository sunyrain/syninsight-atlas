  • The substrate name is copied from a phenoxy compound, but label 5, amount/mmol, forward schemes and product 4 identify the iodinated precursor. This conflict is retained; the name is not used to replace the graph.
  • SI reports calculated 371.9702 for C14H11IO4 but found 369.9703; neutral graph mass is about 369.9702. Original values are retained.
  • SI reports calculated 313.0906 for C16H13N3O4 but found 311.0907; neutral graph mass is about 311.0906. Original values are retained.
  • The 13C list for ketone 6 duplicates the list for chromone 12. That list is not used as independent structure confirmation.
  • SI procedure yields 72.3/77.5/73.4 percent differ from rounded scheme labels 72/78/73; dataset uses procedure values.
  • Article Scheme 1 reports 3 h while the SI scheme says 4 h; procedure includes cooling/addition intervals. A single harmonized duration is not asserted.
  • 6 plus 11 to 12 comprises condensation followed by iodination. No structure is drawn for the unisolated internal species, so no intermediate SMILES is invented.
