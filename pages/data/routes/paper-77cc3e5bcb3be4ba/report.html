来源边界与冲突

  • Only SI is archived. This is a complete transcription of the selected SI preparation network, not verified full-article coverage.
  • Raw native associations incorrectly include annotation-only nodes and reagents as products. Curated endpoints were checked against source images and experimental sections.
  • Unspecified Bpin nodes were converted as carbon by native parsers; pinacol boronate graphs were transcribed from the source drawings.
  • Spurious square-planar sulfonyl sulfur stereo removed by source-figure transcription; carbon stereochemistry retained.
  • Bracketed epoxide 30 is a source-proposed intermediate in 10-to-31, not an independently isolated reaction or additional measured yield.
  • SI writes [M+H]+ but gives a sodium-containing formula and sodium-adduct mass. Both claims are preserved; structure matches the sodium formula.
  • Scheme writes MeOH; experimental paragraph specifies EtOH/toluene. No silent normalization to either condition.
  • Propargylzinc bromide is identified from the source drawing; a unique solution species or canonical organometallic SMILES is not asserted.
  • SI attributes preparation of commercial substrate 15 to reference 1; do not count this as newly established methodology in this article.
