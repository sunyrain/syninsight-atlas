  • 16% and 11% yields refer to the two-stage sequence from 16. Unnumbered aldehyde was isolated at 67% in the text but not drawn; no new intermediate graph is invented.
  • TBDMS branch product 18 is reported partially racemized. Drawing depicts the positive enantiomer but is not an assertion of pure sample; ee is not calculated from optical rotation.
  • Small-scale optimization yields up to 83% are not substituted for the reported 70% preparative yield.
  • Each experiment uses a diastereomeric mixture, not coupling between the two listed molecules. Both source-drawn structures are retained in a single preparation event.
  • Recovered s3 at 41% is stored separately from the main iodination/deprotection path. The bracketed iodinated intermediate is drawn and retained as an operation endpoint.
  • Multi-stage preparations without internal source drawings remain grouped. No intermediate SMILES or numerical yield is inferred.
