来源冲突与范围

  • Only SI is available. The full article route cannot be marked covered.
  • Starting ester 9 and acetal acid 5 are drawn but their preparations are delegated to references; no upstream chemistry inferred.
  • Product 3a on PDF page 9 and starting 3a on page 10 differ at a side-chain stereobond. The product drawing is retained provisionally; the conflicting starting drawing matches the 3b relative pattern.
  • Mixed anhydride, isolated unnamed diazoketone, and homologated acid structures are not drawn in the available SI. Endpoints are recorded as grouped preparation events; internal SMILES and overall yields remain unassigned.
  • Diazoketone yields are 55/70% brsm; following ester yields 88/91% are based on that intermediate. These are not overall yields from 16a/16b.
  • IV/V HRMS lines print precursor-like C32H47N2O8 despite m/z near 297/299. III prints C19H25N2O although its saturated graph requires two more H. Literal formula mismatches are preserved separately from graph-derived mass checks.
  • Several reported found masses differ substantially from graph exact masses (especially 17a-prime). Mass error in ppm is retained; matching formula or valid SMILES is not a certificate of chemical identity.
  • All chiral products are reported racemic. Canonical SMILES represents one source-relative enantiomer; enhanced AND-group CXSMILES and racemate metadata must accompany stereochemical use.
