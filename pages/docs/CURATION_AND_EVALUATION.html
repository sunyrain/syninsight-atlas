Curation and evaluation model

Literature truth

Paper scope, target structure, and route evidence are separate review subjects. Two different experts must accept the same normalized record, or a third expert must adjudicate after two independent submissions. A runnable target requires:

  1. parent paper admitted as a primary completed synthesis;
  2. source-concordant identity and stereochemistry;
  3. a source-bound ordered route or strategic key step.

Automated extraction never settles these claims.

Route value

Route-value review is blinded from paper and system identity. At least three synthetic chemists score route-defining insight, key-transformation plausibility, stereochemical/selectivity logic, convergence/economy, and risk/contingency, followed by pursue, redesign, or stop. Literature similarity is assessed only after the intrinsic-value judgment is frozen.

This design permits a credible novel route to succeed without copying the literature and prevents stock closure or step count from standing in for synthetic insight.
