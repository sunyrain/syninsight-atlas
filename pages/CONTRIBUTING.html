Contributing corrections

Open an issue for public metadata corrections and include the affected paper_id or target_id, the proposed correction, and a DOI/source locator. Do not upload article PDFs, supporting information, screenshots from authenticated sources, or private review forms.

Human admission records are collected through the controlled curation workflow, not through pull requests. Public pull requests may improve the browser, schema, documentation, and reproducible export tooling.
